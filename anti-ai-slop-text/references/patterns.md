# Slop pattern catalog

Contents: 1 Substance, 2 Wording, 3 Sentence patterns, 4 Structure, 5 Formatting, 6 Factual problems, 7 Context-specific notes, 8 Replacement guide.

How to use this: read the text first, then check which of these actually appear. One pattern alone proves nothing; a cluster of them in a text that also says little is the signal. The patterns are examples of a tendency, not a banned-word list. Every phrase below is fine when it carries real meaning in context.

Priority order when fixing: 6 (factual) and 1 (substance) before 2, 3, 4, 5.

---

## 1. Substance problems (fix first)

| Pattern | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Many words, little new information | A long paragraph after which the reader learned nothing checkable | Cut to the facts, or ask the user for them |
| Same idea repeated | "Sangat penting" then "perannya signifikan" then "bernilai besar" | Keep one, the clearest |
| Generic, topic-swappable | The text works unchanged for trains, schools or a Roblox group | Add the detail only this topic has, or cut |
| Everything is a milestone | Small facts framed as "tonggak penting", "babak baru", "a pivotal moment" | State the plain fact and let the reader judge |
| Promotional tone without proof | "Solusi revolusioner yang mendefinisikan ulang masa depan" for a simple app | Say what it does; include the proof the user has, or drop the claim |
| Analysis that restates the fact | "Hal ini menunjukkan pentingnya aspek tersebut dalam konteks yang lebih luas" | Explain the actual cause or consequence, or delete |
| Manufactured consensus | "Para ahli sepakat", "banyak pengamat menilai", "studies show" with no source | Name the source, or say it is the author's view, or delete |
| Off-target content | Facts or sections that do not help what the reader came for | Cut |

Example of the first pattern:

> Teknologi memiliki peran penting dalam perkembangan kehidupan manusia di era modern. Kehadirannya membawa berbagai perubahan yang berdampak pada beragam aspek kehidupan.

Which technology, which changes, what impact? Nothing here can be checked.

## 2. Wording

### Indonesian

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Cliche openers | "Di era digital yang terus berkembang…", "Dalam dunia yang serba cepat ini…", "Seiring berkembangnya teknologi…" |
| Throat-clearing | "Penting untuk dipahami bahwa…", "Perlu diketahui bahwa…", "Perlu dicatat bahwa…" |
| Empty emphasis | "Hal ini tidak dapat dimungkiri…", "Tidak bisa dipungkiri bahwa…" |
| Bombastic vocabulary | fundamental, krusial, signifikan, komprehensif, holistik, esensial, optimal |
| Flowery imagery | "menenun sebuah mosaik yang kaya…", "lanskap yang terus berevolusi", "permadani budaya" |
| Over-formal verbs | menghadirkan, mengoptimalkan, memfasilitasi, mengeksplorasi, memanfaatkan, mengimplementasikan |
| Significance claims | "menjadi bukti nyata pentingnya…", "mencerminkan transformasi…", "menandai babak baru" |
| Stock connectors, repeated | "Selain itu", "Lebih lanjut", "Di sisi lain", "Oleh karena itu" at the start of most sentences |
| Formulaic closers | "Dengan demikian, dapat disimpulkan bahwa…", "Secara keseluruhan…", "Sebagai penutup…" |
| Over-hedging | "Meskipun demikian, perlu dicatat bahwa…", "Hal ini tentu bergantung pada berbagai faktor" |

### English

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Cliche openers | "In today's fast-paced digital world…", "In the ever-evolving landscape of…" |
| Throat-clearing | "It's important to note that…", "It is worth mentioning that…" |
| Bombastic vocabulary | delve, crucial, pivotal, intricate, tapestry, landscape, underscore, showcase, leverage, robust, seamless, testament, realm, multifaceted |
| Flowery imagery | "a rich tapestry of…", "navigating the complexities of…" |
| Significance claims | "stands as a testament to…", "marks a pivotal moment…", "plays a crucial role in shaping…" |
| Stock connectors, repeated | "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally" opening many sentences |
| Formulaic closers | "In conclusion…", "Overall…", "Ultimately, it's clear that…" |
| Over-hedging | "That said, it's worth noting that results may vary depending on a variety of factors" |

Word-level caution: a vocabulary list is weak evidence on its own. What matters is density, many of these in one text, and whether the word says anything. "Krusial" is fine in a sentence about a cracked support beam.

Plain replacements are in section 8.

## 3. Sentence patterns

| Pattern | Indonesian example | English example | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Not only X but also Y" | "Website ini bukan hanya platform digital, tetapi juga ekosistem inovasi." | "It's not just a tool, it's a movement." | Fine once; slop when it recurs and Y is vaguer than X |
| "Not merely X, but Y" | "Komunitas ini bukan sekadar tempat berkumpul, melainkan ruang kolaborasi." | "This isn't merely a team, it's a family." | Often a way to inflate a plain fact |
| Rule of three | "Modern, inovatif, dan berorientasi masa depan." | "Fast, flexible, and future-ready." | Rhetorically valid; artificial when nearly every paragraph has one |
| Forced contrast | "Masalahnya bukan pada teknologi, melainkan pada cara manusia memakainya." | "The problem isn't the tech, it's how we use it." | Legitimate sometimes; a template when constant |
| Tail-clause analysis | "…memperkuat konektivitas, sehingga mencerminkan transformasi digital yang semakin berkembang." | "…, reflecting the broader shift toward digital transformation." | The final clause sounds big and adds no information |
| Rhetorical question then answer | "Apa artinya ini bagi kita? Jawabannya sederhana…" | "So what does this mean? Simply put…" | Fine as a rare device |
| Mirrored triads in headings | "Cepat. Mudah. Aman." for every feature | "Simple. Powerful. Scalable." | Check each word is true and specific |

Fix: say the plain version. "Website ini bukan hanya platform digital, tetapi juga ekosistem inovasi" becomes whatever the site does, such as "Website ini memuat daftar pemain, jadwal turnamen dan klasemen yang diperbarui tiap malam" (only if those are true).

## 4. Structure

| Pattern | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Generic-definition opener | "X adalah sesuatu yang memiliki peran penting dalam kehidupan modern", topic begins paragraphs later | Start at the actual question |
| Identical section mold | Definition, benefits, advantages, challenges, future, repeated whether the topic needs it | Keep only sections the reader needs |
| Too many headings and bullets | Heading every two sentences; every bullet starts with a bold label and a colon | Merge into prose; keep lists for real lists |
| Recap conclusion | A "Kesimpulan" or "Conclusion" section that restates the document | Cut, or replace with the one decision or next step the reader should take |
| Forced "Challenges" and "Future outlook" | Generic speculation attached to simple topics | Delete unless the user has real content for it |
| Uniform rhythm | Same sentence and paragraph length and the same transitions from start to end | Let length follow the content |

Search Engine Land's engagement analysis found a "Conclusion" heading had the strongest negative correlation with reader engagement in their data set, while em dashes alone did not hurt engagement. Treat that as one data point about structure, not a universal rule: a long technical report may genuinely need a closing section.

## 5. Formatting

| Item | When it becomes a problem |
|---|---|
| Em dash (—) | Repeated for drama where a comma or period works. One or two in a long text is normal; not a reason to delete all of them |
| Bold | Nearly every key term bolded with no reason, so nothing stands out |
| Emoji on every heading | In formal or professional documents. In a casual chat or a Discord post emoji can be right; match the user's register |
| Too many headings | The piece reads as a stack of cards instead of flowing text |
| Raw markdown | Stray `**`, `###` or backticks printed in a context that does not render markdown (plain .txt, an email, a chat paste) |
| Symmetric formatting | Every section has the same number of bullets of the same length without a content reason |
| Leftover meta notes | "[insert source here]", "[masukkan data]", "Here is your article:", "Sure! Here's a revised version" left inside the deliverable |

Always strip leftover meta notes and chatbot framing from deliverables. That is never a style choice.

## 6. Factual problems (highest priority)

- Numbers, dates or statistics with no source and no way to check.
- Quotes attributed to a person or organization that cannot be traced.
- Citations or links that do not support the sentence they are attached to, or do not exist.
- Cause-and-effect claims stated as established when they are not.
- Confident detail about the user's own project that the user never supplied.

Handling: flag it, do not silently fix by inventing another number. Either remove the claim, soften it honestly ("sekitar", "menurut data yang kami punya"), or list it under what the user must verify or supply. When Claude is the author, do not generate a claim it cannot ground.

## 7. Context-specific notes

**Informative articles.** Slop looks like: answers a different question than the one asked, long definitions for simple concepts, no concrete example, no number or limit that can be checked, facts that do not serve the reader's goal. Fix by answering the question in the first paragraph and then adding one example with real detail.

**News and profiles.** Slop looks like: promotional description of people or organizations, inflating a small event, "diliput berbagai media" without naming any, a cited source that does not back the claim. Fix by stating what happened, who, when, from which source.

**Academic writing.** Slop looks like: unnecessary academic vocabulary, synonym-swapping to look deep, complex sentences with weak logical links, scientific-sounding claims without evidence, interpretation that the data does not support. A 2024 corpus study in Lingua compared ChatGPT-written academic text with human-written text and found differences such as unusual academic vocabulary, ornate language and synonym substitution in repeated sentence frames. Fix by naming the evidence, tying each claim to it, and preferring plain precise verbs.

**SEO and marketing.** Slop looks like: rigid keyword repetition, many subheadings only to fill a template, "ultimate", "lengkap", "terbaik", "revolusioner" with no proof, the same question answered repeatedly in different wording. Fix by answering once, completely, and supporting any superlative with a fact the user can verify.

**Scripts and narration.** Slop looks like: a hook that announces the topic's importance, a stack of rhetorical questions, "but wait, there's more" filler, a recap outro. Fix by opening with the most surprising or useful concrete thing, then proceeding in order.

**README and technical docs.** Slop looks like: a mission statement, a list of adjectives posing as features, "powerful and flexible" with no example. Fix by stating what it does, the install or run command, and one working example.

**Emails and chat.** Slop looks like: three sentences of pleasantries before the ask, "I hope this email finds you well" as automatic filler, repeated apologies, a summary of what the email just said. Fix by leading with the request or news. Keep real warmth if that is the sender's normal tone.

**Community and Discord announcements.** Slop looks like: "kami dengan bangga mengumumkan", "sebuah langkah besar", "nantikan keseruan tak terbatas". Fix by giving what, when, where, how to join, and what is different this time, in the community's own voice.

## 8. Replacement guide

| Instead of | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Di era digital ini… | (delete; start with the point) |
| Penting untuk dipahami bahwa X | X |
| Hal ini sangat krusial | (say why, with the consequence) |
| memanfaatkan | pakai, menggunakan |
| menghadirkan | punya, menyediakan, bikin |
| mengoptimalkan | memperbaiki, mempercepat (say which) |
| memfasilitasi | membantu, memungkinkan |
| mengeksplorasi | melihat, mencoba, menelusuri |
| komprehensif | (list what it covers) |
| Dengan demikian, dapat disimpulkan bahwa | (delete, or give the actual decision) |
| leverage | use |
| showcase | show |
| delve into | look at |
| a testament to | (state the evidence) |
| In today's fast-paced world | (delete) |
| It's important to note that | (delete, say the thing) |
| plays a crucial role in | (say what it does) |
| In conclusion | (delete, or give the one takeaway) |

The replacement should be accurate to what the author means. When none of the plain options fits, the sentence probably had no content, so delete it.
