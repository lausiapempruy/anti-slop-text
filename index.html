How it is counted

Stock phrase density adds up to 60 points, contrast framing up to 20, leftover placeholders 15, a closing heading 5 and connector openers up to 12. Under 25 is low, under 55 is medium, the rest is high. A text that lists slop on purpose, like a pattern catalog, scores high too.
